# Prototype legal and privacy notes

No model training is supported. Requirements text is sent to the configured embedding provider after client consent; retrieved sources and authorized bounded repository excerpts may be sent to the advisory model.

The readable application notice is at /legal. It distinguishes product terms, privacy practices, project-specific agreements, PayPal's terms, optional sponsor/provider terms, and the original source license. Its date is October 28, 2026. It does not assert compliance with any regulatory regime or establish a governing jurisdiction.

Operator identity, legal contact, support contact, governing-law decisions, deployment regions, production retention schedule and request-handling procedures remain explicit placeholders. Do not invent a business entity or deploy for real personal data collection before establishing those details and obtaining appropriate review.

Users create fixed-role accounts; clients and freelancers explicitly confirm a specification version. Audit timestamps are evidence of application actions, not an automatic legal determination. Revision instructions and acceptance rationales remain recorded. Scope changes after agreement require a new milestone.

Data stored: display names, emails, password hashes, roles, preferences, specifications, uploaded artifacts and hashes, notes, test results, model reports, decisions, payment references and audit metadata. PayPal receives payment-related data. Configured AI receives deliverables for proposals and notes/check summaries for review; retrieved requirement text and authorized repository excerpts are included; raw uploaded evidence file bytes are excluded. Elastic and Zapier use minimal fields with opt-in, described on Settings and /legal. Provider terms, training/data usage, retention and cross-border processing must be checked for the actual configuration.

The local prototype retains data until the operator removes it. There is no self-service deletion, password recovery or production complaint workflow. Revoking Elastic consent excludes results immediately but does not delete prior external index entries. No advertising analytics is included. Deployments must document database backups and external-provider retention.

ProofPay assists requirement structuring, evidence organization, automated checks and payment workflow. AI and checks can be incorrect. Clients make acceptance decisions. No escrow, quality guarantee, payment guarantee, legal dispute resolution, legal advice, compliance certification, endorsement, refund promise or service-level agreement is offered. PayPal sandbox merchant receipt is not a freelancer payout.
